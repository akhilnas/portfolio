# Other ideas

Design and theme ideas for the notebook look that haven't been built yet. Content is out of scope here.

## Smaller touches

- **Taped photos:** project images look taped or paper-clipped onto the page, each tilted at a slightly different angle.
- **Doodled icons:** swap the theme toggle, RSS and social icons for simple pen-style doodles.
- **Lamplight dark mode:** dark mode as the same notebook under a desk lamp, with a warm glow from one corner and dimmer paper, instead of flat navy.
- **Scribbled 404 page:** a torn-out page with "this page was ripped out" scribbled on it.
- **Page numbers:** a handwritten number in the footer corner of each page.
- **Bookmark ribbon:** a ribbon hangs from the top while reading a post and gets longer as you scroll.
- **Sketchy focus outlines:** keyboard focus draws a hand-drawn circle around the link instead of the default dashed outline.

## Motion

- **Headings write themselves in:** section titles appear stroke by stroke the first time they scroll into view.
- **Ink-drop theme toggle:** switching themes spreads out from the toggle like ink soaking into paper, using a same-document view transition.
- **Directional Older/Newer links:** the pager links at the bottom of posts turn the page backwards or forwards to match, for example by adding the `back` view-transition type when "Older" is clicked.

## Keep in mind

- Use the handwritten font (Caveat Brush) only for accents like headings, notes and labels, and keep body text in EB Garamond.
- Every new animation should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
