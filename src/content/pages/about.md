## Akhil Nasser - AI/ML Engineer based in Frankfurt

**Hi there!** I'm Akhil Nasser, an AI/ML Engineer residing in the bustling Financial capital of Germany - [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt). With my unique background and varied experiences across industries along with a love for solving challenging problems, I bring a unique blend of creativity and technical expertise to the world of AI.

## Skills and Expertise

My primary area of focus for the last two years has been in Agentic systems and other scaffoldings around the use of Large Language models. I started my career in Natural Language Processing (NLP) with tasks such as Entity Recognition, Data Anonymization and semantic analysis. Since the arrival of GPT-3 on the scene I have been leading the initiative to make use of this technology within the workflows of Partex.AI, transforming a vast codebase which solves various sub-problems with speciailized approaches to a more general purpose one size fits all solution.

I do not put an undue emphasis on the branch of the artificial intelligence tree the particular method belongs to but rather how good and efficient it is to solve the current problem in question. Certain problems are better solved using traditional ML approaches either due to scale or auditability.
